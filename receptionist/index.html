Straight answers

Do callers know it's an AI?

Yes — it introduces itself as an automated assistant. Honesty converts better than tricks, and several states require disclosure on recorded lines anyway. Most callers just want the problem handled at 9pm; they don't care who types the ticket.

What about my existing number?

Nothing changes. You forward missed/after-hours calls to your AnswerPine number (every phone carrier supports conditional forwarding), or use it as your main line — your call.

What can't it do?

It won't quote exact prices it hasn't been given, won't promise arrival times you didn't authorize, and hands anything weird to a human callback. You define the guardrails in onboarding.

Recording & compliance?

Calls are transcribed for your records; we configure state-appropriate recording disclosures, and texting runs on a registered business line (10DLC) — the legal way.

How fast is setup?

Fill the form today; you get a private test number within 2–3 business days; go live whenever you're happy with how it answers.

How is this different from an answering service?

A traditional service puts callers in a queue for a stranger with a script. AnswerPine answers on the first ring at 3am, knows your services and prices because you configured them, books the job, and texts you the summary — flat monthly rate, no per-minute meter. And anything it can't handle becomes an immediate message to you, not a lost call.
