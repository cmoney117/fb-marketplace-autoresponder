Common Emergencies

We Handle Any Plumbing Emergency

If it's flooding your home, backing up sewage, or leaving you without water — we fix it. Licensed, insured, fixed price.

💧

Burst or Broken Pipe

Turn off your main shutoff valve immediately, then call us. We'll locate the break and repair or replace on the spot.

🌊

Active Water Leak

Hidden leaks inside walls, under slabs, or under sinks — found and fixed with minimal disruption to your home.

🚽

Sewage Backup

Raw sewage coming up through drains or toilets is a health emergency. We respond immediately with the right equipment.

🔥

Water Heater Failure

No hot water or a leaking water heater tank. We diagnose, repair, or replace same-day at a fixed project price.

🏗️

Slab Leak

Leaks under your foundation cause serious structural damage fast. We detect and reroute or repair with minimal concrete work.

🚿

Total Drain Blockage

When every drain in the house backs up at once, it's a main line blockage. We clear it immediately with hydro jetting or snaking.

❄️

Frozen Pipes

Frozen pipes can burst without warning. We thaw safely and inspect for cracks before they become a flood.

⚠️

Gas Line Issue

If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 first. If it's a gas-to-appliance connection issue, call us for licensed repair.

FAQ

Emergency Plumbing Questions

Do you really charge the same price at 2am as during the day?

Yes. We don't have an "after-hours rate" or emergency surcharge. The fixed price we quote you at 2am is the same price we'd quote you at 2pm for the same job. That's our fixed-price guarantee — and it applies to emergency calls too.

How fast can you get to my house?

We dispatch immediately upon quote approval. For most areas of Nashville and Middle Tennessee, arrival is within 45–90 minutes depending on location and current demand. We will give you an accurate ETA when we dispatch.

What should I do while waiting for the plumber?

Shut off the water main if you have a burst pipe or major leak — the shutoff valve is usually near your water meter or in your basement/crawl space. For sewage issues, avoid using any drains or toilets. We'll walk you through steps on the phone when you call.

Are your emergency plumbers actually licensed?

Yes — every plumber we dispatch is licensed in the state of Tennessee, fully insured, and background-checked. You can ask for the license number when they arrive. We don't send handymen or unlicensed technicians for plumbing work.

What if the job is more complex than expected?

If we discover something that changes the scope of work, we stop and quote the new work before proceeding. You always approve any price change before it happens — we will never surprise you with a larger bill than what was agreed.

Do you cover all of Middle Tennessee?

We cover Nashville and all surrounding areas — Brentwood, Franklin, Mt. Juliet, Hendersonville, Murfreesboro, Clarksville, Gallatin, Goodlettsville, Madison, Antioch, and more. If you're in Middle Tennessee, call us — we almost certainly cover your area.
