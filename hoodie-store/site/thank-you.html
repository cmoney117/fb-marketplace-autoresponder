What happens next

  1. If you typed your height at checkout, we check it against the maker’s chart before we place your order. If a different size looks like a better fit, we email you, and you decide.
  2. Arrives 10⁠–⁠22 business days after you order (US, tracked).
  3. We email your tracking link as soon as it ships. It comes from our fulfillment partner, so it may arrive in their packaging rather than a Hunkerhood box.
  4. If it’s going to be late, we’ll email you before your ship-by date with a new date and the option to cancel for a full refund.

Safety

  • Adult garment. Not for children.
  • Never use on or around babies or children under 3. Weighted products are not safe for infants.
  • Only wear it if you can take it off easily on your own.
  • Keep your face and nose clear. Don’t sleep with the hood pulled over your face.
  • If you are pregnant, or have breathing, circulation or sleep problems or limited mobility, check with your doctor before using any weighted product.
  • Keep away from open flames, candles and heaters.
