# Pipeline: Listing Copy Generator

Shared by the POD and digital pipelines. Platform rules baked in from `02-research/04-marketing-and-traffic.md` §1.1–1.2.

## Etsy output package
- **Title:** primary keyword phrase front-loaded, human-readable, no filler/promo words, ≤140 chars.
- **Tags:** all 13, multi-word phrases, top 1–2 keywords repeated from title, remainder spent on synonyms/occasion/recipient/style. No single-word tags.
- **Attributes:** every applicable attribute filled (they act as extra tags).
- **Description:** first 2 lines restate the primary keyword naturally (indexed since 2022); then: what it is → who it's for/gift occasions → specs/sizing → shipping window → care/format details.
- **Flags:** production partner disclosed (POD); AI-generated imagery disclosed (2025+ Etsy requirement); instant download ON (digital).
- **Price:** from the pipeline's margin floor; psychological endings (.99/.95 under $30).

## eBay output package
- **Title:** primary keyword in first 5–7 of 80 chars; brand/spec words after.
- **Item specifics:** every field filled — the most common silent visibility killer.
- **Description:** clean, mobile-first, no external links.

## Own store / Gumroad
- Benefit-led headline, scannable bullets, delivery window + policy links above the fold (chargeback defense), FAQ block (cuts CS volume), UTM-tagged canonical links only from our own channels.

## Quality gate (blocking)
No banned claims (health/safety/income), no trademark terms (IP screen already run upstream), no promises beyond supplier data, delivery window = supplier current +2 days.
