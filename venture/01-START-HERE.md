## The plan in five sentences

Deep research across four business models (full docs + sources in `02-research/`) says the winning combination for "maximum hands-off, minimum investment" is **one Etsy shop running two lanes — digital products and personalized print-on-demand — at $0/month fixed cost, with zero inventory, where Etsy and Gumroad process all payments so your Stripe account is never exposed.** An AI agent runs the shop daily (listings, customer service, order health, Pinterest marketing) and reports weekly; you read a 10-line email on Sundays. Timing matters: the single highest-ranked opportunity is **personalized Christmas ornaments (~80% of sales happen Sept–Dec)** — listings built now index in time for the Q4 wave. Classic dropshipping scored dead last (worst failure data of the four models, needs ~$1,000+/mo in ads, and it's the one model that genuinely endangers your existing Stripe account) — so we're not doing it. Arbitrage (fastest to real profit but needs capital + human sourcing) is fully staged as an optional Wave 2 whenever you want it.
