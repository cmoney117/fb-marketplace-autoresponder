# Claude in Chrome posting brief (paste into Claude in Chrome on your computer)

How to use: open Claude in Chrome on your computer. Claude can't create accounts, so on each setup day create the account yourself first and leave Chrome signed in to it: TikTok @hunkerhood before Day 1, Instagram (and Threads, from that Instagram) before Day 2, the Hunkerhood Facebook Page before Day 5, Pinterest before Day 6 (if @hunkerhood is taken: @hunkerhoodco, then @wearhunkerhood). Facebook stays signed in as you; Claude switches to the Page for Page steps. Before the first run, save the 10 graphics from the phone page into a folder called `hunkerhood` in your Downloads. Each run, change `[N]` on the first line to the day number, then drag that day's graphics (the file names in the day's table) into the Claude in Chrome message along with the brief, so Claude can upload them itself. One day per run.

```text
Today is Day [N] of my Hunkerhood organic test. Hunkerhood is my small online shop: a $99 roomy hooded cloak, free US shipping, 30-day returns, adults only. I'm signed in to my own accounts in this Chrome.

Your job: do that day's posts from my content pack, then reply to comments on my own posts.

1. Open my content pack: https://claude.ai/artifact/ATxoYphy4czdEUfcArGoEL (it's my private page; I'm signed in to claude.ai here). Open that day's card (tap its number in the strip at the top). Read that day's table and boxes, the never list in "Before you start", and "Date rules (check before posting)" in section 3. For every "through Mon 16 Nov" or "after Mon 16 Nov" choice, use today's real date (ask me if you aren't sure of it), not the date printed on the day card or the card the page highlights (those assume I started on Mon 2 Nov), and post the alternative box where the rules say so.
2. Before each post, open that account's profile and check the post isn't already up. If it is, skip it and tell me.
3. Post each box exactly as written: same words, same link, same hashtags, and the graphic files the table names. Don't rewrite, shorten, add emojis or add hashtags. The only swap allowed is the pack's Nashville rule: before the Instagram or Threads bio, the Pinterest About, D5-1 or G-A, ask me whether I live in or near Nashville; if I don't, write "Tennessee" instead of "Nashville" in those places only. Never change the street address (159 4th Ave N, Ste 100 #2179, Nashville, TN 37219). I've attached today's graphics to this message: upload those, in the order the table lists them. Don't click a button that opens my computer's file picker (you can't see that window). If a graphic is missing or the upload fails, stop at that step, tell me the file names in order, and carry on once I've attached them myself.
4. TikTok: post only after my profile's Website shows the TikTok link from the pack. If you can't find or set the Website field on the web, don't post; tell me and I'll check in the app. Before posting, turn on the disclosure setting (on the web it's under "Show more", called "Disclose post content" or "Disclose commercial content") and choose "Your brand"; never post without it. Use only a Commercial Music Library track. Then post the pack's pinned comment under the post and pin it. If the web uploader can't make a photo post with the table's images, or has no Commercial Music Library or no disclosure setting, or the comment has no Pin option, don't work around it (no video, no other sound, no other images): stop, tell me which step is missing, and I'll do it in the TikTok app. TikTok's "Promotional content" label notice and its Music Usage Confirmation are expected, not a warning: show them to me and let me accept them myself.
5. Facebook groups: the day's table doesn't name a group, so before any group post ask me which group to use and wait for my answer (I only pick groups I've taken part in for at least 2 weeks). In that group, open the About / rules first and take a screenshot of them. Post only if the written rules allow promotion, and only on the group's promo day or in its own promo or vendor thread; never as a comment under a member's post and never as a DM. If the rules ban it or don't say, or today isn't the promo day, don't post; tell me instead. At most one group post per calendar week, and group 2 at least 7 days after group 1.
6. Profile setup steps (bio, website link, Page details): do them only on the Hunkerhood accounts (see the first hard rule), using the pack's exact text and the tracking link for that channel. If a step needs a new account, a new Page or a sign-up screen, stop and let me do it.
7. Replies: open the comments on my own posts from the last 2 days, and the DMs people sent me in that time. Answer only with the matching template in "7. Reply templates", word for word, with the one change that section's first paragraph gives: on Facebook (including my group posts) and Threads, point to the link in the post instead of the bio (for example "Leave your email at the link in the post"), or to "our Page" on a Facebook Page post that has no link. Use the versions marked (waitlist) while the shop page still asks people to join the launch list; use the (orders open) versions only once it lets people order. If no template fits, don't reply; list it for me.
8. When you're done, give me a short list: each post with its link, each reply sent, and anything skipped and why.

Hard rules (stop and ask me instead of doing any of these):
- Act only as Hunkerhood. Before any setup step, post or reply, check that the account you're acting as is @hunkerhood, @hunkerhoodco or @wearhunkerhood (on Facebook: switched to the Hunkerhood Page). The only exceptions: a row whose channel is "Your Facebook profile" and the Facebook group rows go from my personal Facebook profile (friends link on the profile, fbgroup link in groups), and the Reddit row goes from my existing Reddit account. If it's any other account, stop and tell me. Never rename another account, switch it to a business or professional account, or change its bio or links.
- Never type a password, a verification code, or card or bank details. If a site asks me to sign in or verify, stop.
- Never spend money: no Boost, Promote, ads, paid verification or subscriptions. If a button would charge me, don't click it.
- Never start a text, email, Messenger chat or DM with anyone, friends included. Rows whose channel is Texts or Email (F1, F2) are mine to send from my own phone and inbox, and Instagram Stories (Days 8 and 12) need the Instagram phone app: skip all of these and list them for me at the end.
- Never comment on other people's or brands' posts, never follow or tag anyone, and never send anyone a first message. The only exceptions are the pack's own: a group post in a group's promo or vendor thread (step 5), the Reddit row's single comment in the weekly promo thread, and replies under my own posts or to DMs people sent me first (step 7). Only post on my own accounts, and in a Facebook group only as step 5 allows.
- Never post the same text in two groups or communities, and never post anything that isn't in the pack.
- Never mention health, sleep, stress, weight, fabric, sizing, delivery dates or holidays beyond what the pack's box says.
- Never show the drawing without its "Illustration" label, and never call it a photo.
- If anything looks off (a warning, a restriction notice, a policy pop-up other than the two TikTok notices in step 4), stop and tell me.
```
