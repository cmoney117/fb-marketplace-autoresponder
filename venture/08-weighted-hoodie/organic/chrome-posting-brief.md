# Claude in Chrome posting brief (paste into Claude in Chrome on your computer)

How to use: open Claude in Chrome on the computer where you're signed in to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and Pinterest. Change the day number on the first line, paste the whole box, and send. One day per run. Before the first run, save the 10 graphics from the phone page into a folder called `hunkerhood` in your Downloads.

```text
Today is Day [N] of my Hunkerhood organic test. Hunkerhood is my small online shop: a $99 roomy hooded cloak, free US shipping, 30-day returns, adults only. I'm signed in to my own accounts in this Chrome.

Your job: do the Day [N] posts from my content pack, then reply to comments on my own posts.

1. Open my content pack: https://claude.ai/artifact/ATxoYphy4czdEUfcArGoEL (it's my private page; I'm signed in to claude.ai here). Go to "Day [N]". Read that day's table and boxes, and the never list in "Before you start".
2. Before each post, open that account's profile and check the post isn't already up. If it is, skip it and tell me.
3. Post each box exactly as written: same words, same link, same hashtags, and the graphic file the table names. Don't rewrite, shorten, add emojis or add hashtags. The graphics are in my Downloads/hunkerhood folder. If you can't attach a file yourself, stop at that step, tell me the file name, and carry on once I've attached it.
4. TikTok: before posting, turn on the content disclosure setting and choose "Your brand". Use only a Commercial Music Library track. Pin the comment the pack gives.
5. Facebook groups: open the group's About / rules first. Post only if the written rules allow promotion; take a screenshot of the rules before posting. If the rules ban it or don't say, don't post; tell me instead.
6. Profile setup steps (bio, website link, Page details): do them on accounts I'm already signed in to, using the pack's exact text and the tracking link for that channel. If a step needs a new account, a new Page or a sign-up screen, stop and let me do it.
7. Replies: open comments and DMs on my own posts from the last 2 days. Answer only with the matching template in "7. Reply templates", word for word (use the waitlist version unless the pack's switch day has happened). If no template fits, don't reply; list it for me.
8. When you're done, give me a short list: each post with its link, each reply sent, and anything skipped and why.

Hard rules (stop and ask me instead of doing any of these):
- Never type a password, a verification code, or card or bank details. If a site asks me to sign in or verify, stop.
- Never spend money: no Boost, Promote, ads, paid verification or subscriptions. If a button would charge me, don't click it.
- Never message, comment on, follow or tag strangers or other brands. Only post on my own accounts and in groups the day's table names.
- Never post the same text in two groups or communities, and never post anything that isn't in the pack.
- Never mention health, sleep, stress, weight, fabric, sizing, delivery dates or holidays beyond what the pack's box says.
- Never show the drawing without its "Illustration" label, and never call it a photo.
- If anything looks off (a warning, a restriction notice, a policy pop-up), stop and tell me.
```
