# Brand Name Screen: Weighted Hooded Cloak

Checked on 2026-10-15. This is a first-pass screen, not legal clearance, and nobody involved is a lawyer. It rules names out cheaply. Before filing a trademark application or spending on the brand, the owner should confirm the recommendation in USPTO TSDR or have an attorney do a clearance search.

## Recommendation: **Hunkerhood**

| | |
|---|---|
| Domain | **hunkerhood.com**: available on 2026-10-15 (Vercel registry lookup, read-only, nothing bought) |
| Say it | HUN-ker-hood. Three syllables, spelled the way it sounds. |
| Meaning | "Hunker down" means to settle in and stay put, usually when the weather turns. "Hood" names the product. Together: hood up, stay in. |
| Screen | **Clear at the first-pass level.** Nobody is using the exact name anywhere we could find. The nearest marks share only the dictionary word "hunker" and are small pending applications (details below). |
| Spare domains, all available, none bought | hunkerhood.co, hunkerhood.shop, hunkerhood.store, gethunkerhood.com, wearhunkerhood.com, hunkerhoodco.com |

**Why this one wins**
- It is the only finalist that passed all three tests at once: the exact name is unused, the .com is free, and it is easy to say and spell.
- It fits the product without making any claim. It says "stay in and get cozy", not "this treats something".
- It works whether or not the sample turns out to be weighted. "Hunker" is about settling in, not about weight, so if the weight claim has to be switched off, the name still fits.
- It avoids every banned name and the sheep/flock/wool family completely.
- It stays away from children's brands and kids' creators. Two of the other candidates collided with Roblox creators; this one did not.

**What to keep an eye on (none of it blocks the name)**
1. **HUNKER & VIBE**, two pending US applications: serial 98353171 (class 25: hats, shirts, sweatshirts; filed 2024-01-11, last seen at "new application") and serial 98530861 (broader goods, with a design element). The only market use we found is a single "Hunker & Vibe Varsity Hoodie" on a vintage home-goods shop, so the brand barely trades. Both marks share the word "hunker", but HUNKER & VIBE is a two-part phrase mark. **Owner action:** look up both serials in TSDR before filing.
2. **HUNKR**, serial 98821765. Pending, filed 2024-10-25, class 25 (shirts, polo tops, caps, footwear) plus weather-information services. It is spelled differently and comes from a weather service, not a cozy-goods brand. **Owner action:** check its status in TSDR.
3. **Hunker** (hunker.com) is a home-design publisher. It sells media, not goods, has reviewed weighted blankets, and reportedly went quiet around 2025. At first, a search for "hunkerhood" may show some Hunker articles. That is a small SEO point, not a trademark one.
4. "Hunker Down" slogan merch is everywhere (Redbubble, Amazon). So we can never own the phrase "hunker down". What we can protect is the whole word **Hunkerhood**. Copy can still use "hunker down" freely.

**Social handles (not verified).** This network blocks Instagram, TikTok, X and Facebook (every direct profile request failed to connect, with no response), so the check had to go through web search. Search found no account called "hunkerhood" on any platform. **Owner action:** open each of these and claim any that are free: instagram.com/hunkerhood, tiktok.com/@hunkerhood, x.com/hunkerhood, facebook.com/hunkerhood, pinterest.com/hunkerhood, youtube.com/@hunkerhood. Fallbacks: @hunkerhoodco, @wearhunkerhood.

## Runners-up

### 1. Duskhood: duskhood.com available (also duskhood.co, wearduskhood.com, duskhoodco.com)
It is the best-sounding name on the list: two syllables, easy to spell, and it suits the cloak look and the end-of-day idea. Nobody sells anything called Duskhood. **It is held back by a nearby home-textile brand:** DUSK (dusk.com) is a UK home-furnishings retailer that sells blankets, faux-fur throws and bedding. It also has a US storefront (us.dusk.com) and a pending US application for **DUSK.COM** (serial 98513055, filed 2024-04-22). In "Duskhood", the word "hood" just describes the product, so DUSK is the part people remember. Against an active blanket seller, that is a real risk. The handle @duskhood on X belongs to a personal account. **Verdict: caution.** Use it only if an attorney says DUSK.COM is not a problem.

### 2. Fernhush: fernhush.com available (also fernhush.co, fernhushco.com)
It sounds soft and calm, and the exact name turned up nowhere. **Held back by two things.** First, it contains the whole word HUSH, and Hush is a weighted-blanket brand: Hush Blankets Inc. has a Canadian registration (2004897, class 24, weighted blankets) and a US application (90202390, suspended as of the last snapshot we saw). Hush Homewear Ltd. also filed HUSH in class 25 in the US (99055885, Feb 2025). Second, FERN has a pending class 25 application (90826570, Cedar Point Holdings: sweaters, hats, jackets). Out loud, it can be misheard as "fern bush". **Verdict: caution.**

## Method

1. **Generate.** The goal was short coined compounds that sound cozy and settled (den, hood, mantle, cloak, nook, moss, fern, ember, dusk, hearth, burrow, hunker), easy to say and spell. Excluded on purpose: Eden, Free the Sheep, Mellow Days, Indio, Arcane, anything sheep/flock/wool, and any word about health or therapy (anxiety, therapy, calm-as-treatment).
2. **Domain pre-screen.** About 200 .com names were run through `mcp__Vercel__get_bulk_availability` (team `team_gnfB7jT0qmJynACDfVkyIKqS`) so effort went into names that were actually available. Then every one of the 15 finalists, and the variant tried for each taken .com, was checked one by one with `mcp__Vercel__get_domain_availability`. These are read-only registry lookups. Nothing was reserved or bought.
3. **Rule for a taken .com.** One natural variant was tried (get-, -co), and the table records which one.
4. **Conflict screen.** For each finalist we ran:
   - an exact-match web search for the name, quoted, plus a "brand clothing" variant;
   - trademark searches for the name and its leading word in classes 25 (clothing) and 24 (blankets/textiles), aimed at Justia Trademarks, uspto.report, Trademarkia and CIPO (Canada) records;
   - a social-handle search through the search engine.
5. **Limits of this screen.**
   - USPTO TSDR, tmsearch.uspto.gov and trademarks.justia.com could not be reached from this environment (DNS and proxy blocked). So trademark data comes from search-engine copies of Justia, uspto.report, Trademarkia and CIPO pages, which can be out of date.
   - Direct profile checks on Instagram, TikTok, X and Facebook were blocked (every request failed to connect, with no response). Handle results come from web search only.
   - Unregistered "common-law" use and non-US registers were covered only as far as web search goes.
   - This screen is not legal clearance.

## Using the name: first copy lines

Keep the brand name in one place in the store config so it can be swapped for a runner-up in one edit: `{{BRAND_NAME}}` = `Hunkerhood`, `{{BRAND_DOMAIN}}` = `hunkerhood.com` (**not bought yet; the owner buys it**).

Safe whether or not the sample is weighted:
- Hood up. Stay in.
- A soft, roomy hooded cloak for staying in.
- "Hunker down" means settle in and stay put. That's the whole idea.

Only if the sample confirms the weight (wire to the central `{{IS_WEIGHTED}}` / `{{WEIGHT_LB}}` flags, never hard-coded):
- A soft hooded cloak with a little weight to it. Feels like a hug.
- About {{WEIGHT_LB}} lb, spread across your shoulders.

Never use: any treatment or health claim (treats, reduces, relieves, therapy, clinically, anxiety relief), "first" or "original", fake urgency or countdowns, invented review counts or star ratings, "as seen in".

## Owner to-do (only you can do these)
1. Buy **hunkerhood.com**. Optionally also hunkerhood.co to block look-alikes.
2. Claim @hunkerhood on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube and X, or the @hunkerhoodco fallback.
3. In USPTO TSDR (tsdr.uspto.gov), look up serials **98353171**, **98530861** and **98821765** and note whether each is live, abandoned or registered.
4. Before filing your own mark (class 25 "wearable blankets; hooded cloaks", and class 24 "blankets" if wanted), get an attorney clearance search, or at least run a TSDR word search for HUNKER*.
5. If anything in steps 2–4 goes wrong, fall back to **Duskhood** (after an attorney looks at DUSK.COM 98513055) or **Fernhush**.
