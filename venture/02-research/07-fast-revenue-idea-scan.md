## 1. The FB Marketplace autoresponder — the honest verdict

**Cannot be sold as-is.** The current extension (DOM-scraping + auto-send on facebook.com) violates [Meta's ToS](https://www.facebook.com/terms/) and [Automated Data Collection Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/automated_data_collection_terms). The environment turned hostile in 2026: **Meta shipped FREE native Meta AI auto-replies for Marketplace sellers (Mar 2026)** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/12/facebook-marketplace-now-lets-meta-ai-respond-to-buyers-messages/)) — our core feature is now a first-party freebie; sellers must pass **government-ID verification**; May 2026 enforcement = 30-day bans with auto-denied appeals; Meta launched its own Seller app (Jul 2026). No official API path for a new micro-SaaS: Marketplace Partner APIs are invite-only; Conversations API covers business Pages, but dealer Marketplace listings have lived on personal profiles since Meta killed dealership listings Jan 2023. Consumer auto-reply extensions are tiny (~280 users on the leading one) — the niche is small, fragile, and Meta-squeezed.

**The compliant pivot that IS sellable:** "FBM Lead Rescue" — extension reads the inbox the dealer is already viewing, **AI drafts replies in a sidebar, the human clicks send in Facebook's own UI**, and all follow-up automation moves **off-platform to SMS/email (Twilio 10DLC)** once the buyer shares contact info. Still sold with honest Meta-risk disclosure. Market is real: FBM remains a top-3 organic used-dealer lead source ([DealerRefresh 2026](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/is-facebook-marketplace-still-one-of-the-highest-roi-lead-sources-for-dealerships-in-2026.13490/)); comps: Podium ~$399-599/mo, VinSolutions $1,500+/mo, CARVID $249/mo, Shiftly ~$1,000/mo. **Price $149-199/mo/rooftop; build 1.5-2 AI-weeks; GTM = DealerRefresh + dealer FB groups + CAN-SPAM-compliant cold email (legal for B2B: accurate headers, physical address, honored opt-out); existing dealer = case study. Sales needs a human ~4-8 hrs/wk.** Decent cash business, not a durable SaaS (Meta AI erodes the wedge).

## 2. Fiverr productized AI gigs — highest certainty, lowest ceiling

AI collapsed the junk tier (Fiverr's stock slide attributed to it — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91581820/fiverr-stock-today-fvrr-freelance-marketplace-collapse-proves-ai-is-decimating-gig-economy-2026-q2-earnings)), but outcome-priced specialist gigs still clear money: **Etsy listing SEO/shop setup, data cleanup, spreadsheet builds** ($75-250 packages). Fiverr takes 20%; AI use allowed (own the rights, disclose if asked). First order: ~2 wks (well-optimized) to 30-90 days typical; median seller makes ~$60/mo (base rate!) — productized specificity + fast delivery is how you beat it. Human is seller-of-record (~3-6 hrs/wk buyer chat); AI does fulfillment. **Authority filter: STRONG PASS — platform supplies trust. Ironic synergy: we sell the exact skills our own Etsy lane exercises daily.**
