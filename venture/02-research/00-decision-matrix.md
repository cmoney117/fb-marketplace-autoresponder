# Decision Matrix — What We Launch and In What Order

Synthesizes the four research docs (01–04). Scores weight the owner's stated constraints: **maximum hands-off** and **minimum upfront/inventory investment** weigh heaviest, then speed to profit, margin quality, account/payment risk, and scale ceiling.

## Scoring (1–10 per criterion, weighted)

| Criterion (weight) | Digital products | POD | Online arbitrage | Classic dropshipping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automation ceiling / owner-hours (25%) — steady-state hrs/wk | **10** (2–5 h) | **8** (3–8 h) | 4 (8–15 h, humans must vet every buy) | 5 (10–20 h, creative treadmill) |
| Startup cost + inventory risk (20%) | **10** ($40–150, zero inventory) | **9** ($65–175, zero inventory) | 4 ($800–2,000, capital converts to inventory) | 3 ($1,500–3,000 mostly ads) |
| Speed to first profit (15%) | 5 (median 8–14 mo to $500/mo; good case 4–6) | 5 (6–12 mo; good case 4–6) | **9** (good case month 1–2) | 6 (days to sales, months-to-never to profit) |
| Net margin quality (15%) | **10** (70–90%) | 5 (10–25%) | 6 (15–25%) | 4 (10–20% if it works at all) |
| Account/payment risk (15%) | **9** (Etsy/Gumroad process payments; Stripe untouched) | 8 (same; IP screening required) | 5 (suspension-happy Amazon, payout holds, inventory stranded if banned) | **3 (Stripe reuse genuinely dangerous; worst failure data)** |
| Scale ceiling (10%) | 7 | 8 | 6 (capital-bound) | 8 |
| **Weighted total** | **8.8** | **7.3** | **5.4** | **4.6** |

## The launch plan this produces

### 🟢 Wave 1 — NOW (August): One Etsy shop running two lanes — Digital + POD
- **Fixed cost: $0/month** (one-time ~$20–30 Etsy setup + $0.20/listing). Payments processed by Etsy/Gumroad — **your Stripe account is never touched.**
- **POD lane (Printify free plan, native Etsy auto-fulfillment):** launch niches from research ranks #1, #2, #5: **personalized Christmas ornaments** (~80% of sales Sept–Dec — August listings index in time for peak; 50–65% gross), **personalized pet memorial art**, **profession micro-cluster apparel**. 20–30 listings per niche.
- **Digital lane (Etsy + Gumroad dual-channel):** research ranks #3, #4, #8: **budget/finance spreadsheets**, **profession-specific small-business template bundles**, **wedding template suites** (operational items, not invites). AI builds the actual products; ~90%+ margins.
- **Why one shop, two lanes:** shared shop-level reputation (reviews, customer-experience score) compounds across both; the same daily agent run manages both pipelines.
- **Volume plan:** 30–40 listings at launch → +5–10/week → 150+ by month 4 (the traffic threshold community data supports).

### 🟡 Wave 2 — OPTIONAL, owner decision (Sept–Dec): Arbitrage cash-flow lane
Fastest path to real dollars (good case: profit in month 1–2) but the *least* hands-off: a human must vet buys and place retail orders. Two ways to run it, owner picks (or skips):
1. **Lean solo test:** $300–500 working capital, used books + Q4 clearance flips on eBay (ungated, no Amazon verification gauntlet), Terapeak-validated (>50% sell-through rule), 1–3 units per new item. ~3–5 owner-hours/week.
2. **VA-driven:** same playbook, a $5–8/hr VA does sourcing/vetting from AI-generated lead lists; owner only funds the buying card. Adds ~$200–400/mo cost; keeps owner near zero hours.
It never touches Stripe (eBay/Amazon pay out directly). Capital risk is real: money becomes inventory.

### 🔵 Wave 3 — AT SIGNAL (validated winners exist): Own store + expansion + ads
Triggers, checked by the weekly review run:
- **Digital revenue ≥ ~$250–300/mo** → launch own store (research: crossover where Stripe's ~4% beats Gumroad's ~14%) on **Cloudflare Pages (free, commercial use allowed)** — NOT Vercel Hobby (its ToS prohibits commercial use). Stripe reuse begins here, with the 0.4% dispute tripwire active from order #1.
- **Any POD niche with 3+ sales/30 days** → expand winners to eBay (250 free listings; new-seller cap ~10 items/$500/mo makes it a winners-only channel).
- **Any product with ≥5 organic sales/30 days** → Pinterest ads at $5–10/day per `05-marketing/ads-system.md` (cheapest CPA ~$7–8, our demographics).

### 🔴 Not launching: Classic dropshipping
Bottom score on the two criteria the owner weighted highest, worst failure data of the four (80–95% by every source), the real cost is $1,000–1,500/mo in ads, and it's the single model where reusing the existing Stripe account is genuinely dangerous (processor freezes documented). If ever revisited, the only acceptable form is the $0/mo eBay + CJdropshipping US-warehouse variant as a Wave-4 experiment — never on Stripe.

## What "success" means (honest targets, from research medians/good cases)

| Milestone | Median expectation | Good case |
|---|---|---|
| First sale | Week 2–8 | Week 1–2 |
| Month 3 | $50–$300/mo profit (waves 1) + arbitrage $150–400 if running | $300–700/mo combined |
| Month 6 | $200–$800/mo | $900–$2,300/mo |
| Month 12 | $500–$1,500/mo | $2,500–$6,500/mo, compounding |

Most sellers quit in the silent first 60 days — the listing machine keeps running regardless, which is exactly why the automated pipeline exists. If month-3 signal is zero across 100+ listings, the weekly review forces a niche pivot (not a quiet death).
