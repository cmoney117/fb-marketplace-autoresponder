# Research: Product & Niche Shortlists (August 2026)

*Research method: 28 searches/fetches across POD, dropshipping, digital products, arbitrage, fees, and validation tooling. Numbers from POD/dropship platform blogs (Printify, AutoDS, CJ, EverBee, etc.) are flagged **[PROMO]** — these companies profit when you believe the niche works. Community-reported figures are **[ANECDOTAL]**. Marketplace/SEC/financial-press numbers are the most trustworthy.*

**Market context to price in:** Etsy is not a growth market right now — FY2025 marketplace GMS declined, active buyers fell 3.4% YoY to 86.5M, and Q4 2025 GMS of $3.29B was up only 0.1% YoY ([Etsy Q4/FY2025 results](https://investors.etsy.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/218/etsy-inc-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results), [EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2026/02/20/etsy-marketplace-shows-flat-growth-in-gms-in-4th-quarter-of-2025/)). You win by taking share in micro-niches, not by riding a rising tide. Etsy's effective fee load is 11–15% of a sale, rising to ~18–20% on offsite-ad-attributed sales once past $10k/yr ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees), [Voolist](https://www.voolist.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026)).

## 5. Validation Methodology — test before committing

**Universal principle:** POD and digital products are natively "list-first, fulfill-later" — a listing costs $0.20 on Etsy and nothing is produced until someone pays. That makes them the correct *first* models. Dropshipping is list-first too (with a compliant wholesale supplier); arbitrage is the only model where cash converts to inventory, so it validates via *data on comps*, not listings.

**POD (per niche):**
1. Pre-validate with EverBee/eRank: pull estimated revenue for top 20 listings on the target keyword; want multiple non-mega shops (under ~1,000 reviews) making sales — proof the niche admits newcomers. Tool numbers are directional, not precise.
2. List 20–30 designs per niche (fewer gives no signal; a viable niche is a cluster of 5–15 keywords).
3. Read signal at 30–60 days: **Kill** if <1% of views convert to favorites/carts and zero sales at 100+ views/listing. **Iterate** at 1–2 favorites per 100 views but no sales (price or mockup problem). **Double down** at 3–5 organic sales in a niche: launch 20+ variants, add personalization, raise prices.

**Digital:** Same mechanics, faster loop — one product per candidate category (1–4 days each with AI), 10–15 listings each across 3–4 categories. 2+ sales/week organically with zero ads = winner; scale into a bundle ladder ($8 single → $25 bundle → $50 mega-bundle). Kill anything with no sale in 60 days after a title/thumbnail refresh.

**Dropshipping:** (a) Order the sample — non-negotiable at 16–20% category return rates; (b) list on eBay/TikTok Shop from a US-warehouse supplier before building any store; (c) test with organic short-form video — judge creatives not products off one video. Kill: no add-to-carts after ~10 videos and 2 weeks.

**Arbitrage:** Validate *before purchase*, every time: Terapeak (free in eBay Seller Hub) — sell-through rate **>50% = buy, <20% = walk away** ([MyListerHub](https://www.mylisterhub.com/articles/ebay-sell-through-rate)); on Amazon, Keepa 90-day history, minimum 30% ROI and $3.50 profit/unit. First buy of any new item type: 1–3 units max.
