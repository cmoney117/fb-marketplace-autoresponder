# Refund & Shipping Policy Templates (per model)

Paste-ready policies. They are deliberately customer-generous: generous policies raise conversion more than they cost in refunds at our price points, and they keep the dispute rate (the metric that can kill the Stripe account) near zero.

## Print-on-demand (Etsy shop policy + own store)

**Shipping:** Every item is made to order just for you. Production takes {2–5} business days, then shipping takes {3–5} business days (US). Full tracking on every order. If we ever miss our estimate, we'll notify you with the option of a partial credit or a full refund — your choice.

**Returns/refunds:** Made-to-order items can't be restocked, so instead of returns we make it right directly: arrives damaged, misprinted, or wrong? Send a photo within 30 days and we'll ship a free replacement or refund you in full — no return shipping, no forms. Wrong size on apparel? We'll replace it at cost. Don't love it? Contact us — we'll find a fair solution; we're a small shop and your review matters more to us than a t-shirt.

## Digital products (Etsy/Gumroad/own store)

**Delivery:** Instant download after purchase. Files: {formats}. Lost the link? Message us any time for a fresh one — forever.

**Refunds:** Because files can't be returned, all sales are technically final — but if the file doesn't work, doesn't match the description, or you bought a duplicate by accident, we'll refund you, full stop. If you're unhappy for any other reason, message us; we're reasonable humans.

## Dropshipping store (own store)

**Shipping:** Orders ship from our US warehouse within {1–3} business days; delivery {4–8} business days with tracking emailed to you. (Only ever list items stocked in US warehouses — long China-direct shipping windows are where dropshipping stores go to die.)

**Returns:** 30-day returns. Defective/wrong item: we pay return shipping or just tell you to keep it — photo is enough. Changed your mind: return unused for a refund of the item price. Every refund processed within 48 hours of approval.

## Non-negotiables baked into all three

1. Delivery windows shown are the supplier's real current windows **+2 days padding** — updated by the automation when supplier windows change.
2. Delay past the promised date auto-triggers the T2 delay email (notify + cancel option) — FTC 30-Day Rule compliance.
3. Refunds ≤$50 are issued same-day without escalation.
4. Policies appear on every listing, the checkout page, and the order confirmation email (chargeback evidence).
