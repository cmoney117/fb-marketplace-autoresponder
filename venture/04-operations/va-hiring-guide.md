# VA Hiring Guide — When and How to Add Humans

**Philosophy:** AI agents do everything they legally/technically can. VAs are hired only for tasks that genuinely require a human: platform actions with no API, judgment calls on escalated customer service, community participation (which must be authentically human — see compliance doc), and physical tasks (arbitrage sourcing, if we go there).

## When to hire (triggers, not vibes)

| Trigger | Hire |
|---|---|
| Customer messages >10/day OR human-flagged drafts >30min/day of owner time | Part-time CS VA (1–2 hr/day) |
| Listing backlog: pipeline generates more products than get published (platform lacks API access) | Listing-upload VA (task-based) |
| Community/social strategy activated (Reddit, FB groups, forum presence) | Engagement VA (must post authentically under a disclosed shop account) |
| Arbitrage wave activated | Sourcing VA (US-based, task-based) or prep-center services |

## Where and what to pay

- **OnlineJobs.ph** — Philippines-based, the default for ongoing roles. E-com VA market rate: roughly **$4–8/hr** for CS/listing work, $8–15/hr for experienced e-com specialists (verify current rates in `02-research/04-marketing-and-traffic.md`). One-time job-post fee model; no agency markup.
- **Upwork** — for one-off specialist tasks (graphics cleanup, video editing) — expect 2–4× PH rates plus fees.
- Pay via Wise/Payoneer (standard for PH), weekly for the first month, then bi-weekly.

## Hiring process (SOP-driven, ~1 owner-hour total; AI drafts everything)

1. AI drafts the job post (template below) → owner posts it (OnlineJobs.ph requires an account — see `06-account-setup/`).
2. Auto-screen applicants: require the word "pineapple" in the application's first line (filters non-readers, ~70% of applicants), a short Loom/voice intro, and answers to 3 scenario questions the AI grades against the SOPs.
3. **Paid test task** ($10–20, 1 hour): give 5 real (or realistic) customer messages + our `customer-service-sop.md`; grade template adherence, tone, and judgment on the $50 refund rule.
4. Hire 2 for a paid trial week, keep 1. Trial = real work with AI double-checking outputs daily.

## Job post template (CS VA)

> **Part-time E-commerce Customer Service VA (US shop, 1–2 hrs/day, long-term)**
> We run US-based online shops (print-on-demand + digital products). You'll answer customer messages using our written playbook (templates provided for every situation), process refunds/replacements under clear rules, and flag anything unusual. Everything is documented — you will never have to guess. Requirements: excellent written English, reliable internet, available to check messages twice daily in US-friendly windows, comfortable following written SOPs exactly. Start the first line of your application with the word "pineapple". Rate: ${X}/hr, paid weekly. Long-term role with raises tied to our shop ratings staying ≥4.8.

## Management (low-touch by design)

- Every task a VA does has a written SOP in `04-operations/` — VAs follow SOPs, they don't invent process. Update the SOP, not the person.
- Daily async check-in (3 lines in a shared doc/Slack: what done, what blocked, anything weird).
- The AI ops agent audits a sample of VA outputs in its daily run and flags SOP deviations.
- KPIs mirror the CS metrics table: response time, rating, refund accuracy.

## Security rules (non-negotiable)

- VAs get **role-limited access only**: Etsy/eBay allow no sub-accounts at small scale, so CS VAs work from a **shared support Gmail** and marketplace message forwarding — never the marketplace login itself where avoidable; where platform login is unavoidable, use a separate browser profile + password manager shared-item (no password disclosure) + no payout/bank pages.
- **Never**: Stripe access, bank access, ability to change payout details, access to the owner's email. No exceptions, including "just this once."
- Offboard = rotate every shared credential the same day.
